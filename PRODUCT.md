# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Purchasing managers, procurement heads, and executives in the rubber and chemical manufacturing sector across Eastern India and globally. They value reliability, established history, and precision.

## Product Purpose
To showcase Usha Commercial Corporation's 55+ years of legacy as a trusted importer, distributor, and indenter of synthetic and natural rubbers and chemicals. It serves as a digital storefront to establish trust, list partners (Global Principals), and capture leads.

## Brand Personality
Industrial, authoritative, precise, established, enterprise-grade.

## Anti-references
- SaaS-style generic landing pages (no cream backgrounds with bento grids).
- Flashy consumer-style e-commerce.
- Overly chaotic technical dashboards.

## Design Principles
- **Heritage Meets Modernity**: Reflect 5 decades of history but through a cutting-edge, premium enterprise lens.
- **Substance over Flash**: Animations should serve the content (e.g., parallax, smooth reveals) without "unnecessary random stuff".
- **Industrial Precision**: Typography and layout should feel engineered and exact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA compliance minimum.
- Clear contrast for readability in industrial/office settings.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
